What this is

This page asks Puter.js (puter.ai.txt2img) to redraw a picture from a prompt. It is a different thing from the rest of this pipeline, which only ever edits a photograph you took and never invents pixels.

Read before you publish with it. The output is an AI-generated image, not a photograph of a print. MakerWorld's "Real life photos" slot asks for a photo of the object you actually printed, and your listing was already pulled once for using someone else's pictures. Use this only if MakerWorld support has told you it is allowed on your account — and be aware a redraw can change the part itself (walls, compartments, layer lines), so the picture may not match what your buyers get from the STL.
No API key is stored anywhere. Puter asks you to sign in to a free Puter account in the browser the first time you generate; your picture is sent to Puter's service.
