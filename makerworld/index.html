Auto · Find and publish

Type a number and it runs the whole job: it pulls that many designs off MakerWorld's licence-filtered listings — only Public Domain, BY and BY-SA can be selected there, so a NoDerivatives or NonCommercial source cannot enter the queue — then for each one downloads the model, cuts the model out of that listing's own photographs and rebuilds only the background, opens the publish wizard in your Chrome, fills every field, declares that listing as the source, and publishes. Nothing asks you per item. Before a picture is used it is looked at against the source photograph by eight independent checks — the model in the new picture is the one that photograph shows, same silhouette, backdrop really changed, backdrop flat rather than half-erased clutter, the model's own colour kept, the model filling the frame, no old background left behind, no words — and one that does not look right is rebuilt on a contrasting backdrop or dropped from the set; it is never posted as the source's untouched file. Measured on 26 Oct over 14 real listing photographs: 6 came out right, so a thin gallery is the normal reason an item stops. An item stops itself only on a fact: no downloadable file, no category named, a licence the form cannot declare (CC0 included — see the note in card 3), fewer than 4 of the source's pictures coming out right, or it is already in the ledger below.

0 · Find models to remix

Reads MakerWorld's own popularity listings (trending / popular / hottest) in a throwaway headless browser — no login, no tab of yours, nothing downloaded or published. Pick one and it drops straight into step 1 as the remix source. These listings are already filtered to the three licences a remix may use (Public Domain, BY, BY-SA), and the filter is read back off the page before anything is listed: if MakerWorld stops showing exactly those three chips green, this refuses to return a list rather than hand you models we may not publish.

1 · Source design

A remix on MakerWorld must name a source that is already a design on MakerWorld — that is the site's own rule, measured from the Model Origin box. A Thingiverse or Printables model can only go out as an original listing here.

2 · Listing

The source's own description is posted unchanged: no added source link, no remix note, no rewritten text. The Find & publish run does the same, automatically.

3 · The listing pictures

Every picture on the listing is drawn here from the model's own name and description, with no words, badges or watermarks in it — and the generator's own corner mark is cut off the bottom of the frame before the file is kept. The other maker's gallery photographs are never attached, and neither are your own. The free generator is thin on capacity: measured on 26 Oct, one picture in about 6 seconds and then a refusal for a minute or two, so a set of five takes up to five minutes. If the set comes back short of what the form needs — this number for the photo slot plus two more for the cover slots — the publish stops and says so instead of quietly using someone else's photo.
The model in these pictures is the one on the source listing: each picture is that listing's own photograph with the model cut out and pasted onto a background built here — the plastic keeps the colour it was photographed in and its geometry is untouched. Before a picture is used it is compared with the source photograph by eight checks (the model in the new picture is the one that photograph shows, same silhouette, backdrop really changed, backdrop clean, colour kept, the model filling the frame, no old background left behind, no words); one that does not look right is rebuilt on a contrasting background, and one that still fails is dropped rather than posted. Needs at least 4 of the source's own pictures — 3 for the photo slot, 1 for the cover — so it refuses a listing with fewer instead of padding the set with drawn ones.

MakerWorld requires at least 3 photos of the model you actually printed, covering 3 views, and says so on the form. The scraped gallery pictures are the other maker's photographs, not yours, and this pipeline's edited renders are covers — neither is sent here. Pick the files that came off your camera.

4 · Check, then publish

Check reads MakerWorld's measured requirements only — no browser, no tab, no draft. Publish opens the wizard in your own logged-in Chrome, fills it, and stops if any read-back disagrees with what the source licence says.
