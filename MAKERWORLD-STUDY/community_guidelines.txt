TITLE: Community Guidelines - MakerWorld

Community Guidelines

Updated: April 27th, 2026

Effective: April 27th, 2026

MakerWorld aims to foster a safe, inclusive, and creative community where users can share 3D printing models, knowledge, and ideas.

These community guidelines clearly define the content and behavior that are not allowed on MakerWorld; they apply to all areas of the platform, including models, print profiles, comments, posts, MakerLab, and related services (collectively, “MakerWorld”).

All users are expected to comply with applicable laws, widely accepted community standards, and these guidelines.

General Guidelines
Illegal Activities and Regulated Goods
Illegal Activities

Content that depicts, promotes, or provides instructions for illegal activities is prohibited, including but not limited to:

Crimes involving personal freedom and safety
Illegal medical or financial activities
Gambling-related activities
Fraud and scams
Cybercrime
Unauthorized data trading
Prohibited or Regulated Goods

Content that promotes, trades, or markets regulated or prohibited goods may be restricted or removed depending on context.

Weapons
Hazardous materials
Drugs and drug-related items
Surveillance or espionage equipment
Any models clearly intended for illegal use
Facilitation of Illegal Activities

Content that provides models, guidance, or services to enable or facilitate illegal activities, or to bypass laws or regulations, is prohibited and will be removed.

 

Violence and Graphic Content

Content depicting violence or graphic imagery is prohibited, including:

Graphic Violence
Graphic depictions of violence, including exposed organs, severe injury, or self-harm and suicide
Depictions or glorification of violent acts or physical harm
Animal Abuse
Graphic depictions, promotion, or encouragement of animal cruelty, torture, or intentional harm
Promotion of tools or models intended for animal abuse

 

Hate Speech and Harassment
Hate Speech and Hateful Behavior

Content that promotes or expresses hatred, discrimination, or exclusion toward individuals or groups based on protected attributes is prohibited, including but not limited to:

Hate speech or discrimination targeting individuals or communities that share protected attributes such as race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, sexual orientation, or disability
Promotion, support, or praise of hateful, extremist, or terrorist ideologies or organizations
Content that justifies, incites, or promotes hatred, violence, or exclusion against protected groups
Harassment and Bullying

Content that targets individuals or groups in a harmful, abusive, or intimidating manner is prohibited and will be removed, including but not limited to:

Insults, harassment, threats, or degrading and demeaning language
Targeted attacks against individuals, including repeated or severe abusive behavior
Bullying, intimidation, or attempts to silence others
Doxxing or sharing personal or sensitive information without consent
Content that encourages or incites harm, harassment, or violence against others

 

Risk and Safety
Physical Safety

Content that may pose a risk to personal or public safety is restricted or removed, including but not limited to:

Promotion, encouragement, or instructions related to self-harm or suicide
Depictions, glorification, or encouragement of dangerous acts that may lead to serious injury or death
Content that provides instructions for the unsafe use, modification, or creation of tools, equipment, or devices that may cause harm
Content featuring devices designed or intended to cause harm or facilitate unsafe activities
Content that encourages reckless behavior or disregards basic safety standards

*Risk Disclosure: Models that may pose risks must clearly disclose these risks for user and reviewer clarity. Disclosure does not guarantee approval or exemption from review, restriction, or removal.

Harmful or Misleading Information

Content that spreads false or misleading information that may cause harm, confusion, controversy, or disruption to individuals or society

 

Nudity and Sexual Content
Explicit Sexual Content

Content involving explicit sexual material is prohibited and will be removed, including:

Pornographic content, explicit sexual acts, or full nudity
Sexual services, solicitation, or promotion of prostitution
Distribution of pornographic materials or links to such content
Any sexual content involving minors
Suggestive and Adult Content

Content that is sexually suggestive or intended for adult audiences may be restricted, including:

Sexualized or suggestive content
Fetish-related content
Adult products intended for sexual use
Partial nudity or content intended to arouse sexual interest

 

Minor Safety

We have zero tolerance for content that harms minors. Such content is prohibited and will be removed, including:

Sexual exploitation or sexualization of minors
Physical, emotional, or psychological abuse of minors
Content that encourages or praises minors engaging in dangerous or inappropriate behavior
Content that distorts or misuses well-known children’s characters or cartoons in inappropriate contexts
Violations of minors’ privacy or personal rights

 

Privacy and Intellectual Property

Content that violates others’ rights may be removed, including:

Privacy Violations
Unauthorized collection, use, or disclosure of personal information
Misuse of personal identity, including images or likeness without consent
Intellectual Property
Copyright, trademark, or patent infringement
Unauthorized distribution or sale of others’ work
Misrepresentation and Impersonation
Impersonating individuals, brands, or official entities in a misleading way

 

Sensitive and Shocking Content

Content that may cause strong discomfort or distress may be restricted, including:

Trypophobia-inducing visuals
Consumption of non-food or harmful substances
Visually disturbing effects
Other shocking content intended to provoke discomfort

 

 

Supplemental Guidelines

MakerWorld is governed by a layered regulatory framework:

General Guidelines constitute the foundational rules applicable to all users across all content types. All community members are required to read and adhere to these guidelines.
Supplemental Guidelines set forth additional rules specific to particular content formats, including but not limited to Models, Profiles, and Ratings.

These two tiers of rules operate on a cumulative basis — Supplemental Guidelines complement rather than supersede the Community Guidelines. When creating, publishing, or engaging with a specific content type, users are obligated to comply with both the Community Guidelines and the corresponding Supplemental Guidelines.The current Supplemental Guidelines are as follows:

Account Policies

These Account Guidelines outline behaviors that are not allowed on MakerWorld. Users must comply with the following rules when creating and using their accounts. Violations of these rules may result in content restriction, removal, account suspension, or other enforcement actions.

 

Account Security

Do not share, sell, trade, or transfer your account, or allow others to access it.

 

Impersonation

Do not impersonate any individual or entity, or misrepresent your identity or affiliation. This includes:

Using another person’s identity or profile information in a misleading way;
Impersonating MakerWorld or other official entities, including using names, branding, or visual elements that may mislead others into believing you are affiliated with them;
Acting on behalf of others without explicit authorization.

 

Cheating & Manipulation

Do not manipulate platform activity or engagement. This includes:

Using bots, automation, fake accounts, or other unauthorized methods to inflate downloads, prints, boosts, likes, comments, followers, or similar interactions;
Offering or exchanging incentives (e.g., rewards, access to files) in return for engagement, such as downloads, prints, or boosts.

 

Spam & Unauthorized Promotion

Do not use your account to spam (e.g., excessive posting or low-quality content), promote excessively, or redirect users off-platform.

 

Abuse of Platform Features

Do not misuse platform features, including the reporting system, to harass, target, or harm others or the community.

 

 

Violations and Appeal
Violations

MakerWorld reserves the right to independently assess the violations of these Guidelines and take the following measures at its discretion:

Remove account from any or all programs
Content takedown
Reduce the visibility of content
Revoke Incomes
Point Penalties
Restriction of Certain Account Functions

Your account may be banned from uploading any models or other submissions.

Your account may be banned from any or all programs applications for 30 calendar days.

Repeat offenders may be suspended from any or all programs applications permanently.

Permanently suspend the account:

All models and profiles under your account will be removed from MakerWorld;

All points associated with the account will be revoked;

The account will no longer be able to post, upload models, or engage in other activities.

 

Appeal

If users believe that restrictions, suspensions, or termination applied to their account have been made in error, they may submit an appeal through MakerWorld’s appeal system or by sending an email to support@makerworld.com. Our teams will conduct a thorough reevaluation of the decision.

Appeal materials must be truthful and complete and should include at least the following information:

The reason provided for the action taken against the account;
A clear explanation of the appeal, briefly describing why you believe the action was taken in error;
Supporting materials such as images, videos, or other evidence that may help clarify the situation and assist in the review process.

Before submitting an appeal, please carefully read and understand the relevant rules. MakerWorld will conduct an independent evaluation based on the submitted materials and existing records and reserves the right of final interpretation regarding the appeal outcome.
