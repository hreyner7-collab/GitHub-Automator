Auto · Find & Publish

Finds models with the licence filter on — CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA ticked, and CC BY-NC / CC BY-NC-SA / CC BY-ND / CC BY-NC-ND / Standard License left unticked (the ticked set is read back before anything is fetched) — most-downloaded first, then re-uploads each one with credit to the designer. Only licences that allow redistribution, remixing and commercial use are published: a NonCommercial source is refused because a listing here can earn, and a No-Derivatives one cannot be remixed at all. Only the useful household families listed above are harvested: a toilet, a bathroom set, garage or workshop gear, a novelty and a decorative trinket can never reach the uploader, and every candidate the gate turns down is listed below with the reason. ND- and NC-licensed finds are skipped and the next model is used; anything that fails is logged with its reason and the run carries on.
